- The paired current-source ten-dataset pre-run had matching generated inputs and 20/20 usable R/Julia fits. Maximum parameter and log-likelihood gaps were `6.11e-7` and `6.79e-10` for that cell. This establishes fit feasibility and close point agreement on ten datasets, not a general recovery rate or calibrated inference. Receipt: `docs/dev-log/cmr/evidence/m5-current-live-pair-prerun-2026-10-20/attempt-001/receipt.md`.
- The paired staged-bundle replay for seed `1800000708` scored both fitted coefficient vectors at fixed quadrature order 41. Independent objectives agreed within `5.01e-12`; the log-SD slopes differed materially, and Julia curvature was unavailable/nonpositive. This diagnoses a flat or weakly identified direction on one input only. It is not coefficient recovery or a broad identification result. Receipt: `docs/dev-log/cmr/evidence/phaseA-replicate-0008-current-input-replay-2026-10-21/attempt-002/RECEIPT.md`.

Together these records support a bounded Phase A recovery diagnosis: a historic admitted screen exposed weak log-SD recovery, while the current paired pre-run supports feasibility on its ten datasets and the single-input replay identifies a weak direction. They do not establish broad current-source parameter recovery. Phase A carries that limitation forward explicitly; it does not relabel a small pre-run as a recovery qualification.
